### Interview Profile: domain-science
Profile-ID: domain-science

Domain emphases — weight probes within the universal axes; never remove axes or lower pass criteria:
- **Unit & dimensional validity:** units, coordinate conventions, and physical-constant provenance for every computed quantity (e.g. hPa vs Pa, geopotential vs geopotential height, ERA5 level mappings, TC tracking thresholds).
- **Surrogate vs. ground truth:** generative surrogate boundaries (cBottle-video start/end conditioning vs prognostic physical dynamics), conservation properties (energy/moisture/momentum leakage), and valid sampling regimes.
- **Numerical stability & conditioning:** tensor representations, zarr store chunking/merging invariants, and degradation regimes.
- **Statistical validity:** sampling independence across 1,000-member ensembles, split leakage, and verification against ground-truth reanalysis.
- **Uncertainty quantification:** what ensemble spread (CRPS, RMSE, spread-skill ratio) does and does not capture across conditioning modes (`start`, `end`, `both`).
- **Reproducibility:** RNG seeds, conditioning masks, environment definitions, and data provenance for all generated figure artifacts.
